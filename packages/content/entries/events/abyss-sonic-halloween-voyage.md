## 🌊 ABYSS: Sonic Halloween Voyage 🏴‍☠️

This isn't your typical Halloween night. It's not just music and drinks. **It's a haunted pirate odyssey, and you're boarding it.** 🏴‍☠️

Squeeze through a crate-lined maze entrance that's only meant for the brave and you land in our **main shipwreck arena**, where sirens watch from the rocks and the old pirate crew didn't quite make it out. 💀🧜‍♀️

### 🔊 THE SOUND

On the decks, expect **bass music: Dubstep, Trap, D&B and everything heavy in between.** But do not worry as we keep it friendly, so you'll find your groove even if you're new to bass.

### 🎭 MORE THAN JUST MUSIC

Between sets, the night comes alive with **roving performers, LED flow artists, pole showcases and butoh dancers.** Browse the pirate market for glow-in-the-dark booths, shopping stalls and candy (the kandi bracelets you know from EDM festivals), and leave your mark on the **blacklight paint wall.** ✨

### 🎶 ARTIST DESCRIPTIONS

#### 🎧 LENERD
Singapore-based EDM DJ who rose through the battle circuit before becoming a five-year resident at Zouk Singapore, performing alongside acts including San Holo, Wuki, Party Favor and ODESZA. He now hosts LENERD+1 at NOVA and is the founder of revelry, a creative collective championing independent artists and rave culture.

#### 🎧 FALLING ISLANDS
Electronic musician, performer and DJ FALLING ISLANDS is the resident DJ of Singapore cybergoth rave collective Blacklight Tribe and an Assistant Tutor at the NUS Electronic Music Lab. Expect a journey through the darker, wilder edges of electronic music.

#### 🎧 SAYVERE
Singapore producer and DJ SAYVERE creates bittersweet, euphoric electronic music built for the dancefloor. His track Rewind landed on Spotify’s New Music Friday across eight countries, while She Said crossed 400,000 plays independently. He has also opened for San Holo and shared stages with MYRNE and WUKONG.

#### 🎧 V4RI
Singapore-based producer and DJ V4RI delivers high-impact sets blending dark techno, EBM, industrial and darksynth with bass-heavy sounds. Expect cinematic soundscapes, hard-hitting grooves and a dark, futuristic edge.
