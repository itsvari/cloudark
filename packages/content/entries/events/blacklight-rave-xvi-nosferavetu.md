**BLACKLIGHT 16: 🩸NOSFERAVETU🩸**  
  
Headquarters Club @ 66A Boat Quay, Singapore 049854

TUESDAY (pre-public holiday) 26th MAY 2026, 10.30PM till 3AM

Come rave with us on World Dracula Day, BlackLight Tribe is back on 26th May with the best bloody rave in SG. Featuring necks-level musical talent from Singapore's industrial cybergoth underground. Polish up those fangs and come slay the night away 🔪🩸🦇🧛‍♂️

DOORS OPEN 10.00pm

**Artist Lineup:**

**10.30pm V4RI**

V4RI is a Singapore-based producer and DJ crafting immersive, high-impact sets that move between dark techno, EBM, industrial, and darksynth, threaded with bass, speed, and electro house, metal-laced drum & bass, and the occasional hyperpop or noise detour. With cinematic sound design and punishing grooves, she shapes tech-noir atmospheres where human desire and machine logic intersect.

**11.30pm FALLING ISLANDS**

FALLING ISLANDS is a Singapore-based electronic musician, performer, DJ, synthesist, educator, and the Resident DJ of Singapore-based cybergoth rave collective, Blacklight Tribe. They also currently serve as the Assistant Tutor to the NUS Electronic Music Lab. Scintillating journeys through the darkest wildest extremities of electronic music are what you can always expect at their shows. Hold on tight.

**12.45am T.HIRST**

T.HIRST is a Singapore based alternative dance producer and DJ known for blending bass music with hardcore and punk. His highly emotional hybrid DJ sets feature live vocals, guitars, drums, and a foot-controlled light show. Think The Bloody Beetroots meets Bring Me The Horizon.

**1.45am D-LINEZ**

D-LINEZ, a Singapore-based DJ and producer, known for her relentless, boundary-pushing "HIGHEST" BPM noise music. But in her upcoming set, prepare for something that dives even deeper—venturing beyond her usual hardcore and uptempo beats. This time, she’s channeling pure, raw intensity, blending rage with haunting screams that sound like your inner demons coming to life. Did someone say Metal Dubstep? Or was it D&B? Maybe Techno? Millennium Hardcore, perhaps? 😉😜

This won’t just be music; it’s an immersive experience that taps into the visceral and the transformative.

This set represents the way of unleashing the chaos, anger, and raw energy that constantly battles within her. It’s a bold expression, allowing those inner voices to roar, transforming her internal noise into something the audience can feel—loud, unapologetic, and real.

The first BlackLight Rave was held on 22nd October 2022. Singapore's music scene had yet to re-emerge from the ashes of covid, and there was no platform that provided a space for musicians that embraced dark themes and genres of sound while pushing the envelope of modern electronic performance art.   
  
BlackLight Tribe is for those that have made peace with their dark side and wish to express it in a safe space that not only accepts their unique oddities, but fetes it.   
  
It is our belief that it's important to embrace the full spectrum of humanity. Aggression, isolation, sexuality, emotional hurt and the various flavors of social maladjustment. Appreciating the beauty that lies within the seemingly dark and macabre. All of these are topics that we should face and normalize.  
  
The interaction that arises from those seeking to understand and those wishing to be understood is where BlackLight Tribe lives and breathes.
