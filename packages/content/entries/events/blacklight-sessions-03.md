BLACKLIGHT TRIBE is inviting the various underground music scenes in Singapore to come and play.

19 levels high in the skyline of Singapore, step into the arena at Tuff Club to watch DJs from the different underground communities in Singapore share their own unique music. Uncurated, undirected, uncensored. This is a freestyle showcase of music straight from the underground.

BlackLight Tribe is a safe haven for various fractals of Singapore's dark alternative communities.

We showcase the incredible talent that can be found in Singapore's underground electronic music scene and provide a space for underground artists to experiment, perform, and grow in their craft.

This event is for partygoers 18yrs and above only.

No illicit substances.

No smoking indoors.

The first BlackLight Rave was held on 22nd October 2022. Singapore's music scene had yet to re-emerge from the ashes of covid, and there was no platform that provided a space for musicians that embraced dark themes and genres of sound while pushing the envelope of modern electronic performance art.

BlackLight Tribe is for those that have made peace with their dark side and wish to express it in a safe space that not only accepts their unique oddities, but fetes it.

It is our belief that it's important to embrace the full spectrum of humanity. Aggression, isolation, sexuality, emotional hurt and the various flavors of social maladjustment. Appreciating the beauty that lies within the seemingly dark and macabre. All of these are topics that we should face and normalize.

The interaction that arises from those seeking to understand and those wishing to be understood is where BlackLight Tribe lives and breathes.
