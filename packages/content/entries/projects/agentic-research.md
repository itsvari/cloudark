## Evidence for technical conversations

During my May to August 2026 Solutions Engineer internship at Cloudflare, I built an agentic AI platform to generate technical presentations for executive and procurement stakeholders.

The platform used evidence-backed research while retaining human review. I demonstrated it at a company all-hands meeting, where it received recognition from executive leadership.

## A research framework that could move

I also built a modular agentic research framework designed to work as a TypeScript module, MCP server, API or OpenCode plugin. It supported dynamic subagent orchestration and autonomous reasoning across multiple threads.

The framework supported proof-of-concept tools for competitor sentiment analysis and modelling prospective customers' technology stacks.

## Practical usefulness

The work brought together AI engineering and technical communication. A result had to be useful to the people discussing it, whether they were technical teammates, internal leaders or external stakeholders.
