## Research into a web application

During my May to August 2025 role as a Software Engineer and Research Assistant at the CENS, S. Rajaratnam School of International Studies, NTU, I developed the Influence Ops Analysis Toolkit.

The public-facing, full-stack application used Next.js, TypeScript and Vercel. The project was demonstrated at academic conferences and to senior government stakeholders.

## Supporting analytical work

Alongside the web application, I built an internal Python visualisation tool using Pandas and Folium. It automated ingestion and geospatial visualisation of submarine cable incidents, generating interactive maps for analysis.

The two pieces of work shared a practical aim: give researchers tools they could use in their existing workflows.

This page is a short portfolio summary of the work recorded in my CV. It describes the implementation and context without reproducing internal research material.
