I'm Ashe Andromeda Low, a Computer Science student at the National University of Singapore. My focus areas are computer security and software engineering. The thread through most of my work is *building things that help other people do their best work*.

In summer 2026, I interned as a Solutions Engineer at Cloudflare. I built an agentic AI platform for evidence-backed technical presentations and a modular research framework that could run as a TypeScript module, an MCP server, an API or an OpenCode plugin. The work combined engineering with the challenge of making technical results useful to different audiences.

Before that, I was a Software Engineer and Research Assistant at the CENS, S. Rajaratnam School of International Studies, NTU. I built the Influence Ops Analysis Toolkit and Python tooling for geospatial analysis of submarine cable incidents. Those projects made *practical usefulness* a concrete requirement: analysts needed tools they could work with.

[Ariadne](/projects/ariadne), my term project, explores how AI mediation affects collaboration in multiplayer VR. I built its TypeScript dual-agent facilitator and the API contracts connecting it to a Unity client. Questions about coherent agents, latency and when a system should intervene are the parts of AI engineering I keep coming back to.

I also lead publicity for the NUS School of Computing AI Society, where my work includes its website, publishing pipeline and visual identity. I was previously President of the NUS Electronic Music Lab. Music has its own home at [V4RI](https://v4ri.xyz), while this site collects the software, projects and working notes.

I also help organise Black Light Tribe, a goth collective in Singapore. These gatherings are part of how I think about friendship and belonging. RICE Media spoke with me about that community in August 2025.
